# 5️⃣ Data Engineering Layer

## 5.1 Cleaning

Tasks:

* Remove HTML tags
* Remove speaker labels
* Remove timestamps
* Normalize whitespace
* Standardize casing
* Detect empty or malformed transcripts

Output:

Clean structured dataframe:

| transcript_id | company | ticker | quarter | year | section | text |

---

## 5.2 Intelligent Segmentation

Do NOT embed full transcripts.

Instead:

Step 1: Separate

* Prepared Remarks
* Q&A

Step 2: Chunk into 500–800 token windows.

Why?

* Improves embedding quality
* Better clustering granularity
* Enables fine-grained retrieval
* Prevents embedding dilution

Each chunk gets:

| chunk_id | transcript_id | company | quarter | section | text |

---

# 6️⃣ Embedding Intelligence Layer

Each chunk is converted into a dense semantic vector.

Baseline model:

* all-MiniLM-L6-v2

Optional domain experiment:

* FinBERT embeddings

Each embedding dimension captures semantic relationships in vector space.

Store:

| chunk_id | embedding_vector | metadata |

Similarity metric:

Cosine similarity:

[
\text{sim}(A, B) = \frac{A \cdot B}{||A|| ||B||}
]

Embeddings enable:

* Semantic search
* Clustering
* Trend comparison
* Cross-company similarity

---

# 7️⃣ Clustering & Theme Discovery

Goal:

Discover latent strategic themes.

Algorithms:

* K-Means
* HDBSCAN
* Hierarchical clustering

Process:

1. Compute embeddings
2. Apply UMAP for dimensionality reduction
3. Cluster in reduced space or full space
4. Evaluate using:

   * Silhouette score
   * Davies–Bouldin index
   * Interpretability

Output:

Cluster labels assigned to chunks.

Example clusters:

* AI investment discussions
* Cost restructuring focus
* Supply chain concerns
* Regulatory risk
* International expansion

---

# 8️⃣ Cluster-Level Summarization

Instead of summarizing each transcript:

For each cluster:

1. Select representative chunks
2. Concatenate key segments
3. Generate high-level executive summary

Example output:

> “This cluster captures increasing capital allocation toward AI infrastructure, automation systems, and cloud scalability across technology firms.”

This produces analyst-ready insights.

---

# 9️⃣ Temporal Intelligence Layer

Since transcripts are quarterly:

We analyze:

* Theme frequency over time
* Cluster distribution by quarter
* Growth rate of AI-related discussions
* Risk emphasis shifts

Example analysis:

* AI cluster frequency in 2021 vs 2024
* Risk discussion spike during recession quarters

This turns static clustering into strategic intelligence.
